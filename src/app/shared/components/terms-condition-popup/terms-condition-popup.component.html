Terms And Conditions
1. Eligibility Criteria

The borrower must be a registered business entity or sole proprietor. Minimum business operation period: 1 year. The borrower must provide valid KYC documents (ID proof, address proof, business registration, etc.).

2. Loan Amount and Tenure

Loan amount shall be sanctioned based on the borrower’s creditworthiness and business requirements. Tenure of the loan ranges from 6 months to 5 years, subject to the bank’s discretion.

3. Interest Rate

The interest rate applicable will be either fixed or floating as per the bank’s policy. Interest rate is subject to periodic review and changes depending on market conditions.

4.Processing Fees and Other Charges

A non-refundable processing fee will be charged at the time of loan application. Late payment charges, prepayment penalties, and other applicable fees will be as per the bank’s norms.

5. Repayment Terms

Repayments will be made in equated monthly installments (EMIs) or as mutually agreed. The borrower must maintain timely repayments; defaults may lead to penalties and legal action.

6. Collateral/Security

The loan may require collateral/security depending on the loan amount and bank policy. The borrower must provide clear title of the security offered.

7. Documentation

The borrower shall submit all required documents including business financial statements, income tax returns, and any other documents requested by the bank.

8. Prepayment and Foreclosure

Prepayment or foreclosure of the loan is allowed subject to prior approval from the bank. Prepayment charges, if any, will be applicable as per bank’s terms.

9. Default and Recovery

In case of default, the bank reserves the right to take legal action to recover dues. The bank may also seize collateral/security to recover outstanding amounts.

10. Other Terms

The bank reserves the right to amend these terms and conditions at any time with due notice. The borrower agrees to provide accurate and complete information and cooperate with the bank during the loan tenure.
